06 / Useful questions

Before you enquire.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Include the property postcode, a short description of the planned work, your preferred timeframe and any photographs that help explain the access required.

Can I enquire about domestic and commercial work?

Yes. Use the contact details below to discuss domestic or commercial scaffolding requirements in Berkshire.

How much will my scaffolding cost?

This preview does not provide prices. Ask L14 for a quotation based on your site, access requirements and intended duration.

What about inspections and site requirements?

Discuss inspection arrangements, permissions, responsibilities and any required documentation directly with L14 before agreeing work. This concept makes no claims about insurance, qualifications or accreditations.
