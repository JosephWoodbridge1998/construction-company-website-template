Before you call

10( Questions ) Useful information before you contact L14.

( Area )Where do you work?

L14 welcomes domestic and commercial scaffolding enquiries across Berkshire. Share your postcode to confirm coverage for your site.

( Projects )Domestic or commercial?

Both. Describe your property or business premises and the work planned so requirements can be discussed.

( Site )What about inspections and permissions?

Discuss permissions, inspection arrangements, responsibilities and any required documentation directly with L14 before agreeing work.

( Photos )Can I send photographs?

Yes. Share useful photographs through WhatsApp or email to help explain the building and access requirements.

( Quote )How much will the scaffolding cost?

Ask L14 for a project-specific quotation. This preview does not publish prices or payment terms.

( Dates )How soon can work start?

Send your preferred dates and expected duration. Availability and arrangements must be confirmed directly with L14.
